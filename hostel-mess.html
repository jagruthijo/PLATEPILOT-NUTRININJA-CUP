Hostel Mess Survival Commandments

1. Eat the Carbs in the Mess

Rice and chapatis in the mess are already paid for in your college hostel fees. Eat your carbohydrates there, and supplement your protein in your room.

2. Keep Desk Staples in Jars

A 1kg container of roasted bhuna chana (₹160) and 500g sattu (₹55) requires zero cooking or refrigeration. Perfect for 4 PM hunger spikes.

3. The Electric Kettle Egg Hack

Place 3 farm eggs inside your electric kettle covered in cold water. Bring to a boil, turn off, let sit covered for 10 minutes. 19g pure protein for ₹21.
