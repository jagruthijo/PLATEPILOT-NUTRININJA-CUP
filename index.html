FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions? We've Got Answers

Is PlatePilot really 100% free with no backend server?
Yes! PlatePilot is built purely on frontend technologies (HTML5, modern CSS3, ES6 JavaScript) and stores all your data securely in your browser's localStorage. No external database, no server tracking, and no hidden subscriptions.
Are the recipe prices realistic for 2026?
Yes! All ingredient costs are calibrated against real 2024-2026 Indian kirana stores and local market prices (e.g. Eggs ₹7 each, Soya Chunks ₹12/100g, Atta ₹40/kg, Rice ₹45/kg). You can even customize unit prices in settings to match your specific campus city!
Can I cook these meals if I only have an electric kettle?
Absolutely. Over 60% of our recipes and snack boosters feature dedicated "Hostel Hacks" for electric kettles, induction pans, or zero-cooking preparation (like Sprouted Moong Chaat, Sattu Chaas, and Roasted Chana).
How does the Meal Optimizer algorithm work?
PlatePilot utilizes a multi-objective greedy & knapsack-inspired optimization function. It systematically scores meal combinations based on strict budget constraints (≤ ₹120), protein density per rupee, calorie matching with Mifflin-St Jeor formulas, and ingredient variety.
